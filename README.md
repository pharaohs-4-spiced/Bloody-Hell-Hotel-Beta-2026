## 🕹️ About

Bloody Hell Hotel is a darkly comedic vampire hotel-management simulation from Unfold Games. Awaken from a centuries-long coma, restore your ruined estate, and build a thriving hotel through exploration, farming, crafting, combat, and resource management. The game is currently in development, and this beta test opportunity gives selected players a chance to help shape the experience and receive a free game key.

Perfect for players who enjoy dark humor, gothic fantasy, first-person exploration, crafting, combat, and detailed management simulations.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🏨 **Restore Your Hotel** — Renovate, upgrade, and expand a ruined vampire estate into a thriving business.
- 🧛 **Play as a Vampire** — Manage hospitality, blood, finances, and the challenges of being an undead hotelier.
- ⚒️ **Gather and Craft** — Forage, farm, mine, and craft the resources needed to rebuild your property.
- 🗡️ **Explore Dungeons** — Fight through monster-infested dungeons to uncover valuable materials and lore.
- 👥 **Hire Staff** — Recruit fellow vampires to handle hotel roles such as cooking, housekeeping, and reception.
- 🌱 **Build Your Operations** — Develop your crypt, underground farm, workshop, kitchen, and other hotel systems.

**A:** Simply sign in with your Steam account, browse available beta programs, request access to games you're interested in, and start testing. After you complete the beta, you'll receive a free Steam key for the full game upon its official release.

**Q: Do I need to pay to join?**
**A:** No, joining a beta test is completely free. All we ask for is your time and genuine feedback.

**Q: Is my Steam account safe?**

**Q: When will I receive the Steam key?**

**Q: Will I get access right away?**
**A:** After you sign in with Steam, your tester profile needs to be approved. Approvals go out as spots free up — roughly once a week. Once approved, beta tests become visible and requestable.

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
